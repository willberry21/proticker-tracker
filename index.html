⏰ How late are the alerts?

A useful signal has to arrive before the move. So for every alert we check the real minute-by-minute prices (including pre-market — trading that happens before the normal 9:30 AM open, where these small stocks often make their whole move) and ask: how much had the stock already jumped earlier that same day when Pro Ticker posted it?

What this is. An independent scorecard for Pro Ticker's daily stock signals. Every completed call is measured against real 5-minute market data from Yahoo Finance and recorded here — win or lose.
Entry price levels are intentionally withheld. This page shows results, not tradable signals: it demonstrates whether the calls work without redistributing Pro Ticker's paid levels.
Best case = a perfect exit at the day's high (nobody does this every time). At close = the honest result if held to the market close. Drawdown = the worst dip after entry. Ran before alert = how much the stock had already jumped that day (pre-market included) before the alert was posted — ⛔ means the day's top was already in.
